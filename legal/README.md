# Nook legal review packet

This folder contains testing/review drafts, not final effective terms. The owner requested placeholders for unconfirmed business and legal details. Do not publish before the operator, audience, jurisdictions, contacts, retention, vendor terms and assent processes are confirmed.

## Canonical documents

- `TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md` and `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` are the editable sources.
- `terms.html` and `privacy.html` are generated review copies.
- Run `python legal/build.py` from the project root after editing the Markdown.
- `INVESTIGATION.md` and `PRIVACY-INVESTIGATION.md` describe source behavior and limitations, not deployed-system certification.
- `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `PRIVACY-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` and `REVIEWER-NOTES.md` identify decisions that require operator/counsel review.

Nook uses CheFu Account for OAuth and the CheFu API for social data and media. Current code does not confirm production provider configuration, retention, backups or legal compliance. No deployment or policy publication is claimed.
